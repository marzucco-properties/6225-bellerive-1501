Property Overview

Renovated, Lakefront, and Genuinely Move-In Ready

A beautifully renovated first-floor end unit in Bellerive at The Vineyards, one of North Naples' established gated communities. Three bedrooms, two full baths, 1,708 square feet, and a screened lanai looking out over the water.

The renovation is what sets this one apart — modern finishes throughout, thoughtfully updated, and turnkey. As an end unit it enjoys added privacy and natural light, and its position beside the community pool puts the water and the lanai within a few steps of each other. The layout is a single-level great room plan with laundry in residence, central air with ceiling fans, and manual storm shutters protecting the lanai. Concrete block construction with a tile roof. One assigned covered carport space, with guest parking nearby.

What makes it work for a tenant is what is already included. The association covers cable, internet, water, sewer, trash, exterior pest control, and lawn, landscape and irrigation care. The tenant pays electric. That keeps the monthly picture simple and predictable.
